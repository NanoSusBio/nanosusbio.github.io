# Research Lines

### 🌍 1. Environmental Technologies & CO₂ Capture
Focused on the development of nanoplatforms for environmental remediation.
* CO₂ adsorption study
* Persistant molecules adsorption study
* Structural-property correlation


### 💊 2. Drug Delivery & Biomedical Applications
* Synthesis of nanoparticles using biogenic route with tea extract
* Functionalization with biomolecules
* Anchoring efficiency of different pharmaceutical molecules


### Nanoplatforms
* CeO₂-based materials: Defect engineering to improve CO₂ activation for adsorption studies and preliminar catalytic tests;
* Mesoporous materials: Functionalization with various natural species for the adsorption of CO₂ and water organic pollutants;
* Synthetic Clay Minerals: Structure modification  to understand relationship with adsorption of organic pollutants.

### Main Techniques
* X-ray diffractometry
* X-ray photoeletronic spectroscopy
* Thermogravimetric analysis
* Scanning electron microscopy
* Transmission electron microscopy
* N₂ Physisorption
* Raman Spectroscopy
* FTIR and UV-vis
