# 🧪 Welcome to NanoSusBio

**NanoSusBio** is an interdisciplinary research group hosted at the **Federal University of São Paulo (UNIFESP)** dedicated to developing advanced nanomaterials for environmental sustainability and biomedical applications.

Our research combines **materials science**, **chemistry**, and **nanotechnology** to address global challenges in carbon capture, catalysis, pollutant remediation, and drug delivery.

# ✨ Why NanoSusBio?

* ✔ **Sustainable Materials** – Focus on eco-friendly synthesis and green chemistry.
* ✔ **CO₂ Capture & Conversion** – Advanced adsorbents and catalytic systems.
* ✔ **Drug Delivery** – Functionalized silica and magnetic nanocarriers.
* ✔ **Advanced Characterization** – XPS, XRD (Rietveld), Raman, TGA, and BET spectroscopy.
* ✔ **International Collaborations** – Joint projects with institutions in Canada and Brazil.
