The **NanoSusBio** (*Nanomaterials for Sustainability & Biomedicine*) is my personal website (Dr. Danilo Losito) created to promote the research lines of my group, and opportunities for young students.
Our work focuses on the surface and structure engineering of nanostructured materials, such as ordered mesoporous silica (SBA-15, KIT-6, SBA-16), synthetic clay minerals (Hectorite) and functionalized metal oxides.

## 🔬 Research Line
### 1. Environmental Technologies and CO₂ capture:
   * Development of porous materials (SBA-15, KIT-6, SBA-16), synthetic clay minerals (Hectorite) and optimized metal oxides for adsorption and catalytic conversion of CO₂ and removal of pollutants.  
* Engineering of structural defects and oxygen vacuities in metal oxides.
* Synthesis of porous materials to remove emerging pollutants in aquatic matrices.
  
### 2. Biomedical Applications and Nanoplatforms for *Drug Delivery*:
* Metal oxide nanoparticles.
* Immobilization and stabilization of biomolecules (enzymes and drugs).
* Nano-regulated systems for controlled release and therapeutic applications.
