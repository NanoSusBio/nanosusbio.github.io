# Welcome

NanoSusBio is an interdisciplinary research group at the **Federal University of São Paulo (UNIFESP)** dedicated to developing advanced nanomaterials for environmental sustainability and biomedical applications.

Our research combines **materials science**, **chemistry**, and **nanotechnology** to address global challenges in carbon capture, catalysis, pollutant remediation and drug delivery.
