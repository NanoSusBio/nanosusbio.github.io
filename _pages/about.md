The **NanoSusBio** (*Nanomaterials for Sustainability & Biomedicine*) is my personal website (Dr. Danilo Losito) created to promote my research lines, and opportunities for young students.
My research is focused on the development of nanomaterials and nanocomposites applied in the environmental and health area.

Research Line
* ** Environmental Technologies and CO₂ capture:** Development of porous materials (SBA-15, KIT-6, SBA-16), synthetic clay minerals (Hectorite) and optimized metal oxides for adsorption and catalytic conversion of CO₂ and removal of pollutants.
* ** Biomedical Applications and Nano-Regulated Systems:** Metal oxide-based nanoplatforms for the loading and release of drugs and biomolecules(*drug delivery*).
